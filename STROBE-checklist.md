**Wording source (verified).** Item wording is from the official STROBE cross-sectional checklist, version 4: https://www.strobe-statement.org/fileadmin/Strobe/uploads/checklists/STROBE_checklist_v4_cross-sectional.pdf (PDF text extracted locally earlier; the PDF is dated October 2007).
